Privacy and Cookies

This page explains what data is stored on your device, the cookie-free analytics we use, and how you control the optional Google Maps content.

Necessary cookies

bar_martiri_language stores your chosen language and bar_martiri_cookie_pref stores your cookie choice. Both are kept for up to 6 months and are used only to make the site work.

Cookie-free analytics

The site uses Vercel Web Analytics for general visit statistics, such as the page visited, referral source, country, and device or browser type. The service does not use cookies and data is processed anonymously, without aiming to identify you personally.

Optional content

Google Maps is not loaded before you consent. If you choose “Accept Google Maps”, your browser connects to Google and their privacy policies may apply. Bar Martiri does not use advertising or analytics cookies on this page.

Managing your choice

You can change or withdraw consent at any time from the “Manage cookies” button at the bottom of the homepage. You can also delete cookies from your browser settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Bar Martiri through the phone number published on the site or the official Instagram profile.
